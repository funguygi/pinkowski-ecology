# Purpose

The Ecology Engine is the living simulation at the heart of Field Station.

It governs every natural process that occurs throughout the institution.

Rather than displaying a static environment, Field Station simulates ecological systems that change continuously through time.

Visitors should feel as though they have entered a real landscape with its own rhythms rather than a collection of webpages.

Nature does not wait for interaction.

It continues regardless of whether anyone is observing.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Ecology Engine follows five principles.

**Everything Changes**

Nothing remains static.

Every visit should feel slightly different.

---

**Everything Is Connected**

Weather affects plants.

Plants affect insects.

Insects affect birds.

Moisture affects fungi.

Light affects everything.

---

**Time Matters**

Every system changes with:

• Hour

• Day

• Season

• Year

---

**Scale Matters**

Large systems influence smaller systems.

Climate

Weather
